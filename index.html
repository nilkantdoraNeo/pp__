Privacy Policy for SwitchSaathi

Effective date: October 29, 2026

SwitchSaathi is a career toolkit that provides salary calculations, job-offer comparisons, technical interview practice, and job planning. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

Information We Handle

You may enter information such as salary figures, job-offer details, interview answers, bookmarks, planner tasks, and personal notes.

Local Storage

The app is designed to store saved information locally on your device. In the current version, this information is not transmitted to a SwitchSaathi-operated server.

You can remove saved information using the app's available deletion controls, your device's app-data settings, or by uninstalling the app. The app does not provide cloud synchronization in this version.

Data Collection and Sharing

The current version is not designed to collect or transmit personal information to our servers. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs. If these features or other third-party services are introduced, this policy will be updated to describe their data practices before they are used.

Internet Access

Core calculators, bundled interview questions, and locally saved features are designed to work offline. Internet access may be needed to download the app, open external links, or access online resources.

Security and Retention

Saved information remains on your device and is subject to your device's security and data-management settings. Avoid saving sensitive information in notes or planner entries. Information may remain on the device until you delete it or remove the app.

Children's Privacy

SwitchSaathi is a general career and productivity tool and is not designed specifically for children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published at this webpage.
